# LinkedIn Outreach Tools: What Users Love and What They Complain About

**GetSales · HeyReach · Expandi · Dripify** (compiled 19 Sep 2026)

Sources: G2, Capterra/GetApp, Trustpilot, AppSumo, TrustRadius, Product Hunt, plus comparison blogs that quote users. All text is paraphrased.

**Frequency:** High = a top theme across several sites. Medium = recurring. Low = a few mentions, or blog-only.

> **About Reddit:** None of the four tools had Reddit threads we could reach directly (Reddit was blocked, and searches returned no threads). The only Reddit evidence is second-hand, cited by competitor blogs, and it is marked as such below. Most comparison blogs are written by competitors (Salesforge, SalesRobot, La Growth Machine, Kondo, LinkedHelper, HeyReach), so their claims are weighted lower than direct reviews.

## 1. GetSales (getsales.io)

### Features Loved

| Theme | What users say | Frequency | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy, intuitive UI | Quick setup, good tutorials; several users call it easier than Expandi or Waalaxy | High | G2 (45 tags), Capterra, Product Hunt, AppSumo |
| LinkedIn + email in one sequence | Both channels run in one workflow, which HeyReach can't do | High | G2 (33 LinkedIn integration, 15 all-in-one), blogs |
| Flexible automation builder | Complex branching; one user said almost any workflow is possible | High | G2 (27), AppSumo, Capterra |
| Responsive support and founder access | Fast replies, Slack support, free calls with the founder | High | G2 (21), Capterra, Trustpilot, AppSumo |
| Account safety | You and the tool can both be logged in to LinkedIn at once; residential proxies; GoLogin integration; switchers report high volume without restrictions | Medium | AppSumo, Product Hunt, blogs |
| Value for money | Cheaper than Expandi for what you get | Medium | Capterra (8), GetApp (value 5.0), Trustpilot |
| Lead-gen results | Dozens of qualified conversations within weeks | Medium | G2 (22), Capterra, Product Hunt |
| Time savings | Automates SDR busywork and follow-ups | Medium | G2 (19), Trustpilot |
| Multi-account / agency management | Sender rotation; many client accounts in one view | Medium | Trustpilot, blogs |
| Unified inbox | One inbox across accounts and channels | Low–Med | Blogs |
| Analytics and A/B testing | Clear data for tuning sequences | Low–Med | Product Hunt, Capterra |
| Waterfall email enrichment | Checks 20+ data providers, with validation | Low | Blogs |
| Built-in light CRM | Useful for basic pipeline tracking | Low | Capterra, Trustpilot |
| API / Make / Clay integrations | Good for building custom stacks | Low | AppSumo |
| Fast development | Active roadmap, frequent releases | Low | AppSumo |

### Features Missing / Issues / Complaints

| Theme | What users say | Frequency | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learning curve | Sequence setup and the settings area are hard for beginners; settings feel less polished than the rest of the app | High | G2 (~27 across tags), AppSumo, blogs |

## 2. HeyReach (heyreach.io)

### Features Loved

| Theme | What users say | Frequency | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-sender rotation | Many LinkedIn accounts run from one dashboard, with volume spread across them. This is its core appeal | High | G2 (27), Trustpilot, TrustRadius, Capterra |
| Unibox (unified inbox) | Replies from every account in one place; no logging in and out; one source says it halved response time | High | Trustpilot, Capterra, TrustRadius |
| Easy, simple UI | Clean and intuitive | High | G2 (21 + 8), Trustpilot, AppSumo |
| Responsive support | Fast and personally involved, **though mostly in older reviews** | High (historic) | G2 (23), Trustpilot, AppSumo |
| Account safety | Built-in limits, human-like delays, a proxy per sender; months with no restrictions | Med–High | Trustpilot, blogs |
| Agency economics | Flat top-tier plans get cost-effective as the number of accounts grows | Medium | G2 (11), Trustpilot |
| Integrations | Clay, HubSpot, Pipedrive, API, webhooks | Medium | G2 (8), Trustpilot, Capterra |
| Quick setup | Up and running in about an hour | Medium | G2 (7), TrustRadius |
| Time savings | Saves hours a day; does work that once needed a team | Medium | G2 (12 + 6), TrustRadius |
| Results at scale | Scalable lead generation | Medium | G2 (7), AppSumo |
| Simple sequence builder | Drag-and-drop action chains | Medium | Trustpilot, blogs |
| Fast development | Frequent new features | Medium | AppSumo, TrustRadius |
| White-label and client workspaces | Suits agencies | Low–Med | Blogs |
| Shared Sales Navigator scraping | Lead lists reused across accounts | Low | Capterra |
| Analytics dashboard | Useful basic metrics | Low | TrustRadius |

### Features Missing / Issues / Complaints

| Theme | What users say | Frequency | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support got worse | Replies slowed from ~30 min to 24–48 h from about Nov 2025; unanswered tickets; some staff called rude | High (in negative reviews) | Trustpilot 1–2★, G2 (2), blogs |
| Bugs and reliability | Campaigns stall or won't start; sequences not saved or lost; fixes create new bugs | High on Trustpilot, Low on G2 | Trustpilot, G2 (2+1) |
| Session and connection drops | LinkedIn sessions expire and need reconnecting; some accounts can't be linked; one user down for a month | Medium | Trustpilot (3–4), Capterra, TrustRadius |
| LinkedIn only | No native email, warm-up, calling or WhatsApp; needs Smartlead, Zapier or Make | High (mostly in blogs) | G2 (2), many blogs |
| Price for small teams | $79 per sender per month; poor value for 1–3 accounts | Medium | G2 (2), blogs |
| Agency plan price rise | A Jan 2026 Reddit post (second-hand) says the 50-account plan went from ~$749 to ~$1,399 a month; enrichment credits are metered | Low–Med | LinkedHelper, citing Reddit |
| Billing and refunds | Strict no-refund policy; one user couldn't reduce seats and kept being billed; some chargebacks | Medium | Trustpilot |
| LinkedIn restriction risk | Restrictions despite safeguards. **On 25 Mar 2026 LinkedIn removed HeyReach's company page and restricted its executives' profiles** (customer campaigns reportedly kept running) | Medium | G2, TrustRadius, HeyReach blog, Wonda |
| Shallow sequence logic | Only an accepted/not-accepted branch; limited A/B testing; no sub-sequences | Low–Med | TrustRadius, blogs |
| Weak reporting | Basic metrics only; no sentiment or ROI views; the AI-assistant connector (MCP) shows numbers that don't match the dashboard | Low | Trustpilot, G2 (1) |
| Limited personalization and filters | Only name-style variables; weak lead filters | Low–Med | G2, blogs |
| Learning curve | Takes longer to master than the marketing suggests | Low–Med | G2 (1), blogs |
| Other gaps | No B2B contact database, no AI reply handling, limited auto-comment, rigid upload-URL rules, inbox UI needs polish | Low | TrustRadius, blogs |
| Easy to misuse | Easy to produce spammy, robotic outreach; unwanted auto-follows | Low | Trustpilot |

| Missing targeting sources | No leads from LinkedIn groups or profile viewers; weak Sales Navigator filters | Low | Capterra |

## Cross-tool patterns (gaps shared across the category)

| Pain point | GetSales | HeyReach | Expandi | Dripify |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn bans despite "safety" claims | ● | ● | ●●● | ●● |
| Per-seat pricing hurts teams | ●● | ●● | ●● | ●●● |
| Bugs, stalled campaigns, lost edits | ● | ●●● | ●●● | ●● |
| Support quality inconsistent or declining | ● | ●●● | ●● | ●● |
| Billing, refund or cancellation problems | · | ●● | ●● | ●● |
| Weak native CRM integrations (esp. Salesforce) | ●● | ● | ●● | ●● |
| Shallow reporting (no sentiment / funnel / ROI) | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| Email as a real second channel | ✅ strong | ❌ none | ◐ basic | ◐ Gmail only |
| Multi-sender rotation and unified inbox | ✅ | ✅ core strength | ❌ | ❌ |
| Editing a sequence that is already running | ◐ | ◐ | ◐ | ❌ |

● = complaint intensity (●●● = major theme) · ✅ strength · ◐ partial · ❌ missing

**Gaps no tool fills well:** reply-sentiment and funnel reporting; editing sequences safely while they run; transparent, fair team pricing with self-serve seat downgrades; reliable session handling (no silent disconnects); and a single LinkedIn + email inbox that shows exactly which sender and step triggered each reply.
